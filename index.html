    I · The Magician · Our craft

    Priming

    Each cover board is sized and primed first, so pigment sits on the surface and does not sink into the cloth. A coat of size and varnish makes the ground.

    Step 1 of 4

    I · The Magician · Our craft

    Sketching

    A loose ink drawing finds the composition. It is drawn fresh for every book, in our own hand, and never traced from official cover art.

    Step 2 of 4

    I · The Magician · Our craft

    Painting

    Gouache and watercolour go on in thin washes, left to bleed softly past the line. The edges of the pages are painted in the same palette.

    Gilding

    Gold leaf is laid by hand on the frame and the title, then the whole book is sealed with a thin coat of varnish.

    XVII · The Star

    The maker

    Opal & Ink is a small studio that rebinds and paints novels by hand. Every book starts as a story someone loves and ends as an object meant to be kept: resewn, cased in cloth, painted, gilded and sealed.

    Each design is drawn from scratch, inspired by the mood of a book and never copied from its official art.

    Questions

    Frequently asked

    Are these official editions?

    No. Every book is an unofficial hand-bound edition made from a lawfully purchased copy, with original artwork by the bindery. Nothing is licensed or endorsed by an author or publisher.

    How long does a commission take?

    Usually three to five weeks from an approved sketch, depending on the design. We will confirm a date before you pay.

    Can you work from a book I already own?

    Yes. Send it to us and we will rebind and paint it. We will agree the design with you first.

    Do you ship outside India?

    For now we ship within India. Nearby countries may follow later.

    How should I care for a painted book?

    Keep it out of direct sun and damp, and dust it with a dry soft brush. Painted edges are sealed but not waterproof.

    Can I return a book?

    Ready-made books can be returned in their original condition within seven days of delivery. Commissions are made for you and cannot be returned, but we will fix anything that is wrong.
